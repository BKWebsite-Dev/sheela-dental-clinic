About the clinic

A neighbourhood clinic that has earned Coimbatore's trust, one careful visit at a time.

Tucked into Sullivan Street in Gandhi Park, right by Town Hall, Sheela Dental Clinic has been a familiar name to families across this part of Coimbatore. What keeps patients coming back — and sending their relatives — isn't a fancy interior; it's the way Dr. Madhusudhan and the team slow down, explain every step, and check in before, during and after a procedure.

That approach shows up in the numbers: a 4.8-star average across 362 Google reviews, with patients repeatedly describing treatments as "gentle," "painless" and "kindly explained." Whether it's a routine cleaning, a same-day emergency, or a full smile restoration, the goal here is simple — dentistry without dread.

  • Transparent, upfront pricing
  • Modern, sterilised equipment
  • Calm, clean, welcoming space
  • Inclusive care for every patient
Meet your dentist

Dr. Madhusudhan, M.D.S.

Founder & Chief Dental Surgeon

Dr. Madhusudhan built Sheela Dental Clinic around a simple idea: patients relax when they understand exactly what's happening in their own mouth. He and his clinical team — hygienists and dental assistants trained alongside him — handle everything from routine check-ups to complex implant and root-canal cases, with an emphasis on minimally invasive, low-pain technique.

Patients frequently mention his habit of pausing mid-procedure to ask "are you comfortable?" — a small habit that shows up again and again in the clinic's reviews.
